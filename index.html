How a repair works

  1. Drop it off

    Come in any time we're open. We look the bike over with you and write up a tag like the one above.

  2. We call if anything changes

    If we find more wrong than the tag says, we ring you with the price before doing the work.

  3. Collect and ride

    We text when it's ready. Pay by card or cash when you pick it up.
